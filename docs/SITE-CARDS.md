# Site cards (draft text)

**Check a bundle in your browser**: Pick a downloaded bundle and have your own browser check every signature, proof and hash in it. The file never leaves your computer: the page cannot make network requests. Status: in progress.

**Dashboard**: Counts of what the ledger holds: by outcome, kind, reason and control, per day, and the newest records. The page recounts the records itself and warns you if its numbers do not match the data. Shows mock data, real signed records. Status: in progress.
