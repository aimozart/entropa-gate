# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions
Date: 2026-10-20. Status: accepted (stage S0b).

## Context
We need a durable record of why the repo is built the way it is, readable by reviewers and by the analyzer of our process (Paxel).

## Decision
Keep one short ADR per consequential decision in docs/adr/ (context, decision, consequences), numbered, never rewritten; supersede with a new ADR.

## Consequences
More files, but every tool or dependency has a reason and a date. Decision outcomes continue to live in a project record (not published).
